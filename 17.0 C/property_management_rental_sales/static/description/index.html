Property Management - Manage Rent/Lease, Sales Contracts

Complete Odoo Property Management System for managing rental, lease, and property sales in one unified platform. Streamline property and unit management, tenant contracts, automated rent invoicing, payment collection, lease renewals, contract settlements, broker commissions, maintenance requests, and property bookings. Fully integrated with Odoo Accounting, CRM, Sales, and Maintenance modules. Perfect for real estate companies, property developers, landlords, and property management agencies looking to automate operations, maximize revenue, and deliver exceptional tenant experiences.

Key Features:

  1. Comprehensive Property & Unit Management

    Manage unlimited properties with multi-unit support. Track property details including type (apartment, villa, commercial), status (available, rented, sold), location, amenities, facilities, specifications, and connectivity options. Support for properties under construction or ready to move.

  2. Flexible Rental & Lease Contract Management

    Create and manage rental/lease contracts with customizable durations, payment terms (monthly, quarterly, yearly), security deposits, and admin fees. Track contract lifecycle from draft to active, settled, expired, or terminated with automated status updates.

  3. Property Sales & SPA (Sale & Purchase Agreement) Management

    Complete property sales workflow with booking management, EOI (Expression of Interest) payments, payment plan configuration, installment tracking, down payment handling, and sale order stages from offer received to registered and sold.

  4. Automated Recurring Invoicing & Rent Collection

    Generate recurring rent invoices automatically based on contract payment terms. Track payment status, send reminders, and manage late fees. Create invoices for security deposits, utilities, admin fees, and other charges with full tax support.

  5. Advanced Tenant & Customer Management

    Maintain detailed tenant profiles including contact details, nationality, profession, visa information, trade license, PO Box, and document expiry tracking. Support for tenant categorization and history tracking across multiple properties.

  6. Broker & Agent Commission Management

    Automatically calculate and track broker commissions with flexible commission types (fixed or percentage-based) and brokerage types (one month or all months). Generate commission invoices and manage payments to real estate agents.

  7. Contract Settlement & Renewal Automation

    Streamline contract settlements with automated calculations for deposits, utility bills, outstanding payments, and refunds. Support for contract renewals with updated terms and automatic notification system for expiring contracts.

  8. Property Booking & Payment Plan Management

    Manage property bookings with configurable payment plans, down payment percentages, booking amounts, and fee structures (admin fee, stamp duty, registration fee, mutation fee). Track booking expiration and payment status.

  9. Integrated Maintenance Request Management

    Seamlessly manage property and unit maintenance requests linked to Odoo Maintenance module. Track work orders, assign teams, monitor repair status, and maintain complete maintenance history for each property.

  10. Real-Time Property Availability Dashboard

    Monitor property portfolio status in real-time with visual dashboards showing available, rented, leased, sold, booked, and under-maintenance units. Track occupancy rates and revenue metrics at a glance.

  11. Full Odoo Accounting Integration

    Seamlessly integrated with Odoo Accounting for automatic journal entries, invoice generation, payment reconciliation, financial reporting, and P&L statements. Support for multi-currency and multi-company operations.

  12. CRM & Sales Pipeline Integration

    Link property inquiries directly from Odoo CRM leads and opportunities to available properties. Track sales pipeline stages from initial contact through negotiation, booking, agreement signing, to final registration.

  13. Utility & Service Charge Management

    Track and bill for property utilities (electricity, water, gas, internet) and service charges. Configure utility products and automatically generate invoices based on consumption or fixed rates.

  14. Detailed Unit Specifications & Attributes

    Define comprehensive unit details including floor/level, building/block, area (sqft/sqm), bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchen, living, dining, balconies, parking spaces, facing direction, and furnishing type (fully furnished, semi-furnished, unfurnished).

  15. Installment & Payment Plan Configuration

    Create flexible payment plans for property sales with customizable installment schedules, payment milestones, and automated invoice generation. Track installment payments and outstanding balances.

  16. Multi-Currency & Multi-Company Support

    Manage properties across multiple companies and currencies. Support for international real estate operations with automatic currency conversion and company-specific pricing and policies.

  17. Advanced Reporting & Analytics

    Generate comprehensive reports on occupancy rates, revenue analysis, contract expiry tracking, payment collections, commission calculations, and property performance metrics with customizable dashboards.

  18. Document Management & Audit Trail

    Store and manage all property-related documents including contracts, tenant KYC documents, licenses, agreements, and brochures. Complete audit trail with activity tracking and change history for compliance.

  19. Landlord & Developer Portal

    Dedicated portal for landlords and developers to track their properties, view revenue reports, monitor tenant contracts, and manage property portfolios with role-based access control.

  20. Geo-Location & Map Integration

    Store property GPS coordinates (latitude/longitude) for location-based searches and mapping features. Enhance property listings with accurate geographical information.
