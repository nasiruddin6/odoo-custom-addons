Employee Leave Summary Report

Employee Leave Summary Report provides a clear overview of employee leave balances, used leaves, pending requests, and remaining entitlements in one place. Easily track annual, sick, casual, and unpaid leaves to ensure accurate HR planning, payroll compliance, and workforce transparency. Ideal for HR teams to monitor leave trends, improve attendance management, and support data-driven decisions.



Key Features:

  1. Date Range–Based Tracking

    Analyze employee leave summary records for a selected start and end date.

  2. Employee wise filter

    Select employees to generate leave summary reports for specific individuals or groups.

  3. Show current leave balance

    On reports show current leave balance of employees for better leave management.

  4. Leave summary Metrics & Analytics

    • Badge ID – Unique employee identification number for accurate leave tracking
    • Employee – Name of the employee who requested or used leave
    • Department – Department or team the employee belongs to
    • Start Date – Leave starting date for the requested period
    • End Date – Leave ending date for the requested period
    • Leave Type – Category of leave such as annual, sick, or casual
    • Total Leave – Total number of leave days allocated or requested
    • Leave Balance – Remaining available leave days after deduction
    • Status – Current leave request status (Approved, Pending, or Rejected)

Description

1. Report Menu Navigation enables quick and intuitive access to HR reports, allowing users to easily view, filter, and analyze employee leave data from a centralized reporting menu.

2. Open wizard to select employees and date to date filters, status (All, To Approve, Second Approve, Approved, Refuse)

3. Employee Leave Summary Report provides a clear summary of an employee’s leave summary for a selected period, including Badge ID, Employee, Department, Start Date, End Date, Leave Type, Total Leave, Leave Balance and Status.
