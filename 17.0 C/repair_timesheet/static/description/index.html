Repair Timesheet Management

Repair Timesheet Management streamlines technician labor tracking directly from repair orders. Easily log work time with start/stop timers or manual entries, manage multiple technicians per repair, and compare planned vs actual hours. Automatically convert billable labor into sales orders and invoices, generate technician timesheets from estimated hours, and include complete technician and timesheet details in repair prints and quotations for accurate billing and full transparency.


How it works? Click here to watch video


Key Features:

  1. Repair Order Timesheet Integration -

    Log technician work time directly from repair orders.

  2. Start / Stop Labor Timer-

    One-click timer to accurately track repair labor hours.

  3. Multiple Technicians per Repair -

    Assign and track multiple employees on a single repair job.

  4. Manual & Automatic Time Entry -

    Support both timer-based and manual timesheet logging.

  5. Planned vs Actual Time Tracking -

    Compare estimated labor time with real execution.

  6. Repair Job Notes & Work Description -

    Add detailed work logs for every labor entry.

  7. Auto Technician Labor Sales Order & Invoicing -

    Convert billable labor into sales order & invoice lines automatically.

Description

1. Set Repair Timesheet Product that automatically converts logged Technicians hours into sales orders and invoices, ensuring accurate billing, faster invoicing, and improved `revenue tracking.

2. Generate Technician Timesheet button automatically creates timesheet entries for selected technicians based on estimated hours, allowing users to easily adjust them later to reflect actual time worked.

3. Repair Order print includes technician details and complete timesheet entries with work descriptions for clear tracking and transparency.

4. Create a quotation directly from the repair order that automatically includes timesheet products with technician hours and pricing on the sales quotation, sales order, and invoice for accurate billing.
