CRM Client Visit & Employee Attendance Management

CRM Client Visit & Employee Attendance Management streamlines field operations by combining CRM visit planning with GPS-based check-in/check-out and automatic attendance generation. It enables teams to schedule and track client visits, verify locations in real time, auto-create HR attendance, and analyze employee performance all from a single, mobile-ready, multi-company compatible solution fully integrated with Odoo CRM and HR.


How it works? Click here to watch video


Key Features:

  1. CRM-Based Client Visit Scheduling -

    Plan and manage client visits directly from leads and opportunities.

  2. GPS Check-In & Check-Out Verification -

    Ensure authentic visits with real-time location tracking.

  3. Automatic HR Attendance Creation -

    Field visits automatically generate employee attendance records.

  4. Smart Visit Workflow Management -

    Track visits from planning to completion with approval control.

  5. Complete CRM Integration -

    Access visit history, outcomes, and next actions from customer records.

  6. Role-Based Access & Audit Logs -

    Secure data visibility with manager override and full traceability.

  7. Mobile-Ready & API-Driven -

    Optimized for field teams with seamless mobile app integration.

  8. Multi-Company Support -

    Manage visits and attendance across multiple companies effortlessly.

  9. Advanced Reporting & Dashboards -

    Monitor employee performance, visit duration, and productivity.

Description

1. Click “Schedule Visit” from the opportunity to initiate a field visit linked to the sales pipeline.

2. Define visit details, assign team, and confirm scheduling.

3. The system prepares a visit confirmation email with all visit details click “Send” to notify the client.

4. Begin visit with GPS check-in and track activities in real time.

5. Click “Check Out” to record visit completion, calculate duration, and trigger automatic attendance creation.

6. Employee attendance is created with check-in/out times and duration visible under the Attendance tab.

7. View actual check-in/out timestamps and GPS coordinates for location verification and audit

8. Log visit result, interest level, expected value, and follow-up actions under the Outcome tab.

9. Upload unlimited files, photos, signed forms, documents, and visiting cards under the Attachments tab.

10. View internal notes and client feedback under the Remarks tab for future reference and quality tracking.

11. Go to “Reporting > Visit Analysis” to view performance metrics and visit summaries.

12. Analyze visit completion trends using bar charts for quick visual insights.

13. Review visit data by expected value, interest level, and actual duration in pivot format.

14. Track visit history with reference, client, team leader, visit type, and status in tabular format.

15. Enable “CRM Visit Manager” or “CRM Visit User” roles in user settings to control access and permissions.
